A.I Objective
The objective of this Annex is to present a database for classification stresses (see Table A.I) considered
to be representative of material that is available to the Canadian market.
A.2 Definitions
The following definitions apply in this Annex:
Note: The following definitions provide a guide to the column headings in Table A.I. The abbreviations in
parentheses following the defined terms reflect the abbreviations in the column headings. See also Note 4) to Table
A.I.
Length — the nominal length of poles in the sample. The testing span is smaller and generally in
accordance with ASTM D1036.
Modulus of elasticity (MOE) — the average modulus of elasticity in 69 bar (103 lbs/in2) units calculated,
in most cases, in accordance with ASTM D1036.
Modulus of rupture (MOR) — the average modulus of rupture or maximum breaking stress in pounds
per square inch.
Note: Users should not infer that all poles broke at the groundline. Many broke at a smaller cross-section where
the calculated extreme fibre stress was usually less, but might have been greater, than that at the groundline
cross-section.
Reference number (Ref. no.) — the number assigned in this Annex's bibliography (see Clause A.9) to
the document on which the related information in Table A.I is based.
Standard deviation — a statistical measure of variability.
Sl — the standard deviation of the modulus of rupture found in the sample.
S2 — the standard deviation of the modulus of elasticity found in the sample.
Site number (Site no.) — the site or pole yard from which the sample was obtained (see Table A.2). For
some sites, additional identifying information can be found in the applicable document referenced in
Clause A.9.
Treatment — the code (see Clause A.8) describing the conditioning and treatment of poles before
strength testing.
Note: Further information can be found in the applicable document referenced in Clause A.9.
