Note: For southern yellow pine, the average number of annual growth rings measured on the butt in the outer 2 in
of poles having a circumference of 37.5 in or less at 6 ft from the butt, and in the outer 3 in of poles having a
circumference of more than 3 ft at 6 ft from the butt, shall be not less than 12 and 18 rings, respectively. However,
pole butts with 8 to IO and 12 to 15 rings, respectively, shall be acceptable if 50% or more latewood is present.
As an alternative, these ring count and latewood measurements may be made on an increment core taken at 6 ft
from the butt directly above the place where the average number of annual rings is indicated on the butt surface.
