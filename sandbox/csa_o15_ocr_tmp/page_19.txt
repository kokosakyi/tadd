4.3 Permitted defects
The following defects shall be permitted:
a)
checks that result from normal seasoning conditions and that extend not deeper than the depth of
pith (see Clause 4.4.1 for limitations on two or more checks that become contiguous at the pith);
b) fire-kill, provided that there is no char;
c) firm red heart; and
d) sap stain that is not accompanied by decay.
4.4 Limited defects
4.4.1 Contiguous checks, through-checks, and splits
Contiguous checks, through-checks, or splits in the butt surface shall be permitted, provided that their
height from the butt along the side surface does not exceed 0.61 m (2 ft).
4.4.2 Compression wood
The outer 2.54 cm (1 in) of all poles shall be free of compression wood visible on either end.
4.4.3 Dead streaks
A single sound dead streak, not wider than one-quarter of the circumference of the pole measured at
the widest point of the dead streak, shall be permitted in cedar poles only.
4.4.4 Decay in knots
Decay shall be permitted in knots, provided that it is not associated with heart rot.
4.4.5 Defective butts
Hollowing of the butt caused by "splinter pulling" in felling the tree shall be permitted, provided that
the area of such a hollow is less than 10% of the butt area. Hollow heart and decay shall be permitted in
cedar poles only, provided that the aggregate area of hollow heart and decay does not exceed 10% of
the butt area and that the depth of the hollow does not exceed 0.61 m (2 ft), measured from the butt
surface. Decay in the butt within 5.1 cm (2 in) of the pole surface shall be permitted in cedar poles only,
provided that the total area of decay does not exceed 6.45 cm2 (I in2).
