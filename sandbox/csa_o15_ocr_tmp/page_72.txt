Scots and southern yellow pines are not native to Canada. Southern yellow pine is the name for a commercial grouping of species that includes loblolly
(Pinus taeda), longleaf (P. palustris), shortleaf (P. echinata), and slash (P. elliottii) pines.
Spruce species include black (Picea mariana), Engelmann (P. engelmannii), red (P. rubens), Sitka (P. sitchensis), and white (P. glauca) spruce, of which only
the latter has been tested in a pole size. Separation of red and black spruce from white spruce is impractical once the pole is removed from the forest.
Small, clear bending specimens tested for each species indicate that white spruce is the weakest of the five.
