3 Definitions
The following definitions apply in this Standard.
Bird holes —
holes and damage caused by woodpeckers and other birds.
Check — a lengthwise separation of the wood that usually extends across the annual rings, commonly
resulting from stresses set up in wood during seasoning.
Compression wood — abnormal wood usually formed on the lower side of branches and the concave
side of leaning trees.
Note: It is characterized by an unusually high proportion of apparent latewood in the annual rings, with a gradual
transition from earlywood (springwood) to latewood (summerwood). It has a lifeless appearance and exhibits a
darker colour when wetted. Compared to normal wood, compression wood shrinks excessively lengthwise.
Crack —
Cross break crack — a separation of the wood cells across the grain. Such breaks can occur when a
tree is felled, during handling, or be due to internal strains resulting from unequal longitudinal
shrinkage.
