6.2 Sawing
Wood utility poles and reinforcing stubs
Poles shall be sawed neatly at the top and at the butt along planes that shall not be out of square with
the axis of the pole by more than 10% of the diameter of the sawed surface. There may be bevelling at
the edge of the sawed butt surface not wider than 10% of the butt diameter.
6.3 Trimming
Completely overgrown knots rising more than 25 mm (1 in) above the pole surface, branch stubs, and
partially overgrown knots shall be trimmed close. Completely overgrown knots less than 25 mm (1 in)
high need not be trimmed.
6.4 Framing
All poles shall be roofed, gained, and bored as specified by the purchaser before full-length treatment.
All gains of a notch or slab type shall be cut on the face of the pole, and the gained surfaces shall be in
parallel planes.
6.5 Markings
6.5.1 Butt marking
The following marks shall be stamped or branded legibly on the butt end of each pole or be included on
a metal tag affixed thereto:
a)
the supplier's code or trademark;
b) code letters denoting the pole species (see Clause 6.5.3); and
c)
the class and length of the pole.
Marks stamped or branded on the butt end shall be not less than 16 mm (5/8 in) high. Marks on metal
tags shall be not less than 3.2 mm (1/8) in high.
6.5.2 Side marking
6.5.2.1
Not more than one month before treatment, the following marks, not less than 16 mm (5/8) in high,
shall be branded legibly and permanently on the face of each pole:
the supplier's code or trademark;
b) the plant location and the last two numerals of the year of treatment;
c)
code letters denoting the country of origin (for imported poles) and pole species (see Clause 6.5.3);
d) the class and length of the pole; and
e) following the species code letters [see Item c)], the appropriate code letter to denote the type of
preservative, as specified in the CAN/CSA-080 Series of Standards.
Note: Subject to agreement between the supplier and purchaser, die-stamped metal tags affixed to the pole face
may be used in place of brands to designate side marking information. If metal tags are used, the code letters
specified in Item c) should be not less than 1/8 in high.
6.5.2.2
The bottom of the side mark shall be located on the face of the pole at 3.05 m ± 0.05 m (IO ft ± 2 in)
from the butt of poles 15.2 m (50 ft) or less in length, and at 4.27 m ± 0.05 m (14 ft ± 2 in) from the butt
of poles 16.8 m (55 ft) or more in length. In the case of 3.05 m (10 ft), 3.35 m (11 ft), and 3.96 m (13 ft)
stubs, the mark should be 0.31 m (1 ft) from the top.
