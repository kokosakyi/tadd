4.4.9 Shakes in butts
Shakes in the butt end surface extending through an arc of not more than 900 shall be permitted.
Shakes extending through an arc of more than 900 shall be permitted if they are inside a circle, the
centre of which corresponds to the centre of the butt surface and the diameter of which corresponds to
one-half of the average butt diameter.
4.4.10 Shakes in tops
Shakes in the top end surface shall be permitted in poles that are to be given full-length preservative
treatment, provided that the diameter of the shake does not exceed one-half of the diameter of the top
of the pole.
4.4.11 Spiral grain
Spiral grain (twist grain) shall be permitted within the limits specified in Table 2.
4.4.12 Crooks and sweep
Crooks other than short crooks shall be permitted in poles. Sweep shall be permitted, provided that a
straight line connecting the midpoint at the groundline with the midpoint at the top shall not, at any
intermediate point, pass through the surface of the pole. Double or reverse sweep shall not be
permitted in reinforcing stubs. (See Figures I and 2.)
4.4.13 Rate of growth
4.4.13.1
Except as specified in Clause 4.4.13.2, the rate of growth measured on the butt in the outer 51 mm
(2 in) of poles having a circumference of 0.95 m (37.5 in) or less at 1.83 m (6 ft) from the butt, and in
the outer 76 mm (3 in) of poles having a circumference of more than 0.95 m (37.5 in) at 1.83 m (6 ft)
from the butt, shall be not less than 6 rings per 2.54 cm (1 in).
4.4.13.2
Poles with 4 and 5 rings per 2.54 cm (1 in) shall be acceptable if 50% or more summerwood is present.
4.4.13.3
As an alternative to Clause 4.4.13.1, the ring count and summerwood measurements may be made on
an increment core taken at 1.83 m (6 ft) from the butt directly above the place where the average
growth is indicated on the butt surface.
