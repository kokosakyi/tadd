5 Dimensions
5.1 Poles
5.1.1 General
Wood utility poles and reinforcing stubs
Poles shall be classified according to species, length, and minimum circumferential measurements as
specified in Tables 4 to 13.
5.1.2 Length
Poles 45 ft in length or shorter shall be not more than 76 mm (3 in) shorter or 76 mm (3 in) longer than
the nominal length. Poles 15.24 m (50 ft) or more in length shall be not more than 76 mm (3 in) shorter
or 152 mm (6 in) longer than the nominal length. Length shall be measured between the extreme ends
of the pole.
5.1.3 Circumference
The minimum circumferences at 1.83 m (6 ft) from the butt and at the top, for each length and class of
pole, are listed in Tables 4 to 13. The circumference at 1.83 (6 ft) from the butt of a pole shall be not
more than 178 mm (7 in) or 20% larger than the specified minimum, whichever is greater. The top
circumferential requirement shall apply at a point corresponding to the minimum length permitted for
the pole.
5.1.4 Classification method
After the species and length of a pole are determined, its class shall be determined by measuring
circumferences at two cross-sections: the top and 1.83 m (6 ft) from the butt. The latter dimension shall
determine the class of the pole by reference to the applicable table in Tables 4 to 13, provided that its
top (measured at the minimum length point) is large enough. Otherwise, the circumference at the top
shall determine the class, provided that the circumference at 1.83 m (6 ft) from the butt does not
exceed the specified minimum by more than 178 mm (7 in) or 20%, whichever is greater.
5.2 Reinforcing stub circumference
The minimum circumference at 1.83 m (6 ft) from the butt, for each length and class of stub, shall be as
specified in Tables 14 to 23.
6 Quality of work and finish, markings, handling, storage, and inspection
6.1 Bark removal
6.1.1
The outer bark shall be completely removed from all poles. No patch of inner bark more than 25 mm (1
in) wide and 152 mm (6 in) long shall be left on the pole surface between the top and 0.61 m (2 ft)
below the groundline.
6.1.2
In shaving poles, the depth of cut shall be not more than necessary to remove inner bark. There shall be
no abrupt changes in the contour of the pole surface between the groundline and top sections.
