CAN/CSA-015-15
C.4 Shrinkage
Wood utility poles and reinforcing stubs
The dimensions specified in Tables 14 to 23 are the minimum dimensions of the poles when offered to
the purchaser, regardless of the state of seasoning. While it is recognized that some shrinkage will occur
in seasoning, it is known that the dimensional loss is offset by an increase in fibre strength.
