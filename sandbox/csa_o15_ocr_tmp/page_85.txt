Note: For southern yellow pine, the average number of annual growth rings measured on the butt in the outer
5 cm of poles having a circumference of 95 cm or less at 1.8 m from the butt, and in the outer 7.5 cm of poles
having a circumference of more than 90 cm at 1.8 m from the butt, shall be not less than 12 and 18 rings,
respectively. However, pole butts with 8 to IO and 12 to 15 rings, respectively, shall be acceptable if 50% or more
latewood is present.
As an alternative, these ring count and latewood measurements may be made on an increment core taken at 1.8 m
from the butt directly above the place where the average number of annual rings is indicated on the butt surface.
