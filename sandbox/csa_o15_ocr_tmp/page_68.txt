Annex E (informative)
Wood utility poles and reinforcing stubs
SI (metric) versions of Tables 1 to 23
Note: This informative Annex has been written in mandatory language for users working with Sl units. Users of this
Standard should note that pole lengths given in Sl units differ slightly in length from their imperial counterparts.
E.I
Tables E.I to E. 23 provide Sl (metric) equivalents for Tables 1 to 23.
The dimensions specified in Tables 4 to 23 have been converted to Sl units in Tables E.4 to E.23 by
recalculating the theoretical circumference at 1.83 m (6 ft) from the butt in yard/pound units. The
results have then been converted to centimetres and rounded to the nearest centimetre. This is
thought to be more accurate than converting the values in Tables 4 to 23, which are rounded to the
nearest 13 mm (1/2 in).
In cases of dispute, the dimensions specified in Tables I to 23 should govern.
