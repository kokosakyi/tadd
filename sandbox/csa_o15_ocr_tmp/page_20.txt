4.4.6 Insect damage
Wood utility poles and reinforcing stubs
Insect damage consisting of holes 1.6 mm (1/16 in) or less in diameter shall be permitted. Surface
scoring or channelling shall be permitted. All other forms of insect damage shall be prohibited.
