species of pole. The code letter for the country of origin shall precede the code letters for the species

6.6 Handling of poles and reinforcing stubs
6.6.1
Treated material shall not be dragged along the ground.
6.6.2
Cant hooks, pole tongs, or other tools shall not be applied to the groundline section of any pole.
